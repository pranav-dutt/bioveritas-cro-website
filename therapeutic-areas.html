Domains

Where our scientists work.

Eight therapeutic and product domains, supported across in vitro, in vivo, bioanalytical, and clinical service lines under one quality framework.

01

Oncology

Solid-tumour and haematological models, cytotoxicity and combination screening, tumour-growth inhibition and PK/PD endpoints.

02

Metabolic disease

Diabetes, obesity, dyslipidaemia and NAFLD models with glycaemic, lipid, and body-composition readouts.

03

CNS & neuroscience

Behavioural, cognitive, and neuroinflammation models with exposure–response and biomarker support.

04

Inflammation & immunology

Acute and chronic inflammation, autoimmunity, and cytokine profiling across in vitro and in vivo systems.

05

Dermatology & topical

Skin irritation, sensitisation, wound-healing and permeation studies for topical and transdermal products.

06

Infectious disease

Antimicrobial and antiviral susceptibility, MIC/MBC determination, and efficacy models where appropriate.

07

Nutraceutical & AYUSH

Standardisation, safety, and efficacy substantiation for botanicals, extracts, and traditional formulations.

08

Food, cosmetics & consumer

Safety substantiation, claim support, and comparative performance testing for consumer and personal-care products.

Model selection

Disease relevance decides the model — not the other way round.

Every model is chosen against the clinical question: target expression, disease mechanism, and the endpoint that will inform the next decision. Where a standard model does not fit, we develop or adapt one.
