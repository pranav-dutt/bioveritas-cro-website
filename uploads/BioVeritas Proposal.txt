What This Proposal Covers
This is the final, streamlined scope for the BioVeritas CRO website. Two existing pages absorb content that does not
need its own URL: About Us folds in Why BioVeritas, What Makes Us Different, Scientific Philosophy, Integrated
Development Approach, Customised Study Design, Global Partner Network, Quality Commitment, and Translational
Science as long-form sections; Therapeutic Areas includes the 6-stage Scientific Approach infographic. Translational
Research remains a standalone service page. Based on the latest feedback, four further simplifications have been
made: Partner With Us and Collaboration are combined into a single page; Careers and Internships are combined
into a single static, template-based page rather than a dynamic listing system, since hiring volume does not justify
one; Scientific Publications is folded into Resources & Downloads as a section rather than its own page; and
Knowledge Centre is parked for a later phase rather than built now. This brings the site to 29 pages at a total
investment of Rs. 65,000 + GST = Rs. 76,700, issued on behalf of Rabiro Marketing Agency.

SCOPE 4 · PARTNER & CAREERS | RS. 6,000 + GST
Based on the latest feedback, this scope has been simplified from four pages down to two. Since BioVeritas will not be
hiring extensively in the near term, a dynamic careers system is not justified — a single, well-templated static page
covers openings and internships without the ongoing overhead of a custom post type. Partner With Us and
Collaboration are combined into one page, since both are fundamentally about how an external party works with
BioVeritas — commercially or academically — just presented as two clearly separated sections.
Partner & Collaboration (1 Page)
One page, two sections: the three commercial engagement models (full-program partner, milestone specialist,
white-label CRO support) and a separate section for academic/institutional research collaboration (joint studies,
co-authored publications, university tie-ups). One combined enquiry form with a dropdown to route the enquiry to
the right team.
Careers & Internships (1 Static Page)
One template-based page listing current openings and internship opportunities side by side, with a single apply form
(resume upload included). Openings are edited directly in Elementor rather than through a dynamic admin panel —
the right trade-off given low hiring volume, and easy to upgrade to a dynamic system later if hiring picks up.
