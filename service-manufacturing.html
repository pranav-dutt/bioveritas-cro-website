Most products do not fail at the bench — they fail on the way to the plant, when a process that worked in a 2 kg batch meets a 200 kg one.

We prepare the transfer package that carries process knowledge from development into manufacturing: master formula and batch records, critical process parameters, in-process controls, and the analytical methods that will release the product.

We also coordinate site selection and qualification, exhibit and validation batches, and the licensing route with CDSCO and state authorities so approval and manufacture stay on the same schedule.

Transfer knowledge, not just documents

Every transfer package identifies critical process parameters and critical quality attributes explicitly, so the receiving site knows what must be controlled and why.

What we deliver

Support spans partner-site coordination, validation, licensing, and post-transfer troubleshooting.

01

Technology transfer

Transfer protocols, master formula records, batch manufacturing records, and process knowledge documentation for receiving sites.

02

Contract manufacturing coordination

Site identification, capability assessment, technical audits, and ongoing coordination of pilot, exhibit, and commercial batches.

03

Scale-up & process optimisation

Scale-up strategy, equipment-train mapping, and process refinement to hold performance from laboratory to commercial scale.

04

Process & cleaning validation

Validation protocols and reports, sampling plans, hold-time and cleaning validation, and acceptance-criteria justification.

05

Licensing & approvals

CDSCO and state FDA applications, product permissions, test licences, and responses to authority queries.

06

Analytical method transfer

Comparative and co-validation transfer of release and stability methods to the receiving quality-control laboratory.

How an engagement runs

Transfer engagements are milestone-driven and run jointly with the receiving site.

01 · Scoping

Readiness assessment

Review formulation, process, analytical package, and site capability against target scale.

02 · Design

Transfer plan

Define critical parameters, batch sizes, sampling, acceptance criteria, and licensing route.

03 · Execution

Trial & exhibit batches

Support pilot, exhibit, and validation batches on site with in-process oversight.

04 · Analysis

Comparability review

Compare product quality attributes across scales and resolve deviations.

05 · Reporting

Transfer & licence package

Deliver the transfer report, validation documentation, and licence application set.

Quality & data integrity

Work is conducted under quality-managed SOPs with ALCOA+ data-integrity principles. Raw data is captured contemporaneously, archived, and made available for sponsor and agency audit.

  • ISO 9001 quality-management system across the lab's operations.
  • Independent review of protocols, conduct, and final reports before release.
  • Traceable records — every result traces back to an explicit method and standard.
  • Confidential by default — mutual NDA available within 24 hours of request.
