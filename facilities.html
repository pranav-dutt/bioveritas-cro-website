Capability

Instruments, models, and the oversight that connects them.

BioVeritas operates a hybrid model: core science in-house, specialised regulated capacity through audited partner facilities, with a single BioVeritas study director accountable throughout.

01

Analytical laboratory

Chromatography and spectroscopy for method development, validation, release, and stability testing across dosage forms.

02

Bioanalytical capability

LC–MS/MS and ligand-binding assay capability for regulated bioanalysis in plasma, serum, blood, urine, and tissue.

03

Cell culture & in vitro suite

Primary cells, immortalised lines, 2D/3D culture, and plate-reader based functional and cytotoxicity assays.

04

Animal facility access

CPCSEA-registered facilities with IAEC oversight for rodent and non-rodent efficacy, PK, and safety studies.

05

Clinical site network

Access to GCP-experienced clinical sites and ethics committees for Phase I–IV and BA/BE studies.

06

Sample management

Controlled-temperature storage, chain-of-custody documentation, and monitored freezers with excursion alerting.
