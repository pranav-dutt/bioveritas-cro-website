Development That Survives Scale-Up

Formulations are designed with manufacturability in mind from the outset, reducing the surprises that derail technology transfer.

What we deliver

NPD is stage two of our product development pathway. It connects to analytical, safety and clinical evidence stages, and to manufacturing handover.

Formulation development
Oral solid dose, liquids and topicals designed around stability, manufacturability and, where applicable, dissolution and bioavailability considerations. Parenteral formulation is available through a qualified partner.
Preformulation
Solubility, stability, and excipient-compatibility studies to guide formulation.
Prototype & scale-up
Prototype manufacturing and scale-up support toward pilot and commercial batches.
Comparative analysis
Head-to-head characterisation against reference products.
Technology transfer
Documentation and support to transfer processes to manufacturing partners.

Nutraceutical & botanical development

Nutraceutical formulation, botanical standardisation, ingredient selection, prototype development, sensory optimisation, stability, shelf-life, packaging compatibility, contract manufacturing coordination, scale-up, technology transfer and clinical/claim substantiation, where applicable.

Botanical standardisation
Botanical ingredient selection and standardisation based on relevant marker compounds, specifications, identity, purity and quality attributes.
Safety
Safety assessment is based on the specific ingredient, dose, route, duration, target population and available scientific and regulatory evidence. Natural origin is not treated as evidence of safety.
Ingredient vs finished product
Existing ingredient evidence can inform product development and scientific positioning; finished-product claims should be supported by evidence appropriate to the specific formulation and intended claim.
Clinical evaluation
Clinical evaluation and claim substantiation support, where appropriate.

Standards & methods

Formulation and analytical work is documented for reproducibility and to support downstream regulatory filings.

How an engagement runs

A consistent five-stage flow keeps every engagement aligned with regulatory intent, from feasibility through a decision-ready report.

01 · Scoping

Feasibility & Gap Analysis

Map existing data to your target submission; identify gaps, endpoints, and study-design options.

02 · Design

Protocol & Plan

Draft an OECD/ICH-aligned protocol with a statistical/analysis plan and clear acceptance criteria.

03 · Execution

Conduct

Studies run through qualified and appropriately accredited/GLP-compliant partner facilities, as applicable, with BioVeritas scientific coordination and sponsor-facing oversight.

04 · Analysis

Review & Interpret

Quality-reviewed data and documentation, biostatistics where relevant, and scientific interpretation, not just raw delivery.

05 · Reporting

Decision-Ready Report

A structured report and data package prepared with audit and agency review in mind.

Quality & data integrity

Work is conducted under quality-managed SOPs with ALCOA+ data-integrity principles. Raw data is captured contemporaneously, archived, and made available for sponsor and agency audit.

  • Qualified partners: regulated work is conducted through qualified and appropriately accredited partner facilities, as applicable to the study.
  • Independent review of protocols, conduct, and final reports before release.
  • Traceable records: every result traces back to an explicit method and standard.
  • Confidential by default: mutual NDA support, typically within 24 hours.
