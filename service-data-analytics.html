A study is only as strong as the analysis plan written before the first sample is collected — and only as usable as the dataset it leaves behind.

Our statisticians and data scientists design the analysis alongside the protocol: endpoints, estimands, sample size, randomisation, and handling of missing data agreed up front rather than reconstructed afterwards.

We also build the data layer — case-report forms, validation rules, and analysis-ready datasets — so that the same numbers appear in the report, the dossier, and the publication.

Pre-specified, not post-rationalised

Statistical analysis plans are finalised and version-controlled before database lock, keeping conclusions defensible under sponsor and agency scrutiny.

What we deliver

Analytics support runs from protocol-stage design through submission-ready datasets and post-launch evidence.

01

Biostatistics & study design

Sample-size justification, randomisation schemes, estimand framing, and statistical analysis plans aligned to ICH E9.

02

PK/PD & exposure modelling

Non-compartmental and compartmental analysis, exposure–response modelling, and dose-justification support.

03

CDISC data standards

SDTM and ADaM dataset creation, controlled terminology, and define documentation for regulated submissions where applicable.

04

eCRF, eSource & ePRO design

Electronic data-capture design, edit checks, data-management plans, and query workflows that keep databases clean in-flight.

05

Real-world evidence

Retrospective, registry, and observational study design and analysis to support effectiveness, safety, and lifecycle claims.

06

Reporting & dashboards

Tables, listings, figures, statistical report sections, and sponsor-facing dashboards for enrolment, quality, and safety signals.

How an engagement runs

Data engagements run alongside the scientific study rather than after it.

01 · Scoping

Analysis feasibility

Clarify endpoints, decision criteria, and the regulatory or publication target for the analysis.

02 · Design

SAP & data model

Author the statistical analysis plan, dataset specifications, and capture design before first subject in.

03 · Execution

Capture & cleaning

Run edit checks, query resolution, and blinded data review through to database lock.

04 · Analysis

Statistics & modelling

Execute the pre-specified analysis with validated, version-controlled code.

05 · Reporting

Outputs & datasets

Deliver TLFs, statistical report sections, and archived datasets with reproducible code.

Quality & data integrity

Analytical code, datasets, and outputs are version-controlled with independent verification of derived variables and key results.

  • Double programming of primary endpoints and key derived datasets.
  • Version control across SAP, specifications, code, and outputs.
  • Audit trail retained for every dataset transformation.
  • Data privacy — de-identification and access control by default.
