Overview
In vitro studies form the scientific foundation of successful drug development. By evaluating a compound's biological activity before animal studies, researchers can identify promising candidates, eliminate ineffective molecules, and better understand therapeutic mechanisms while reducing development risk.
At BioVeritas, we design fit-for-purpose in vitro studies tailored to your research objectives rather than applying a standardized testing approach. Our scientific teams select appropriate biological models, validated assay platforms, and relevant endpoints to generate reproducible data that supports informed go/no-go decisions throughout discovery and preclinical development.
Whether supporting lead optimization, mechanism-of-action studies, safety assessment, or translational research, our focus remains on producing high-quality scientific evidence with clear biological interpretation and regulatory-ready documentation.
Study Highlights
• Study Design: Protocols developed around your target biology, molecule type, therapeutic indication, and project objectives.
• Relevant Biological Models: Selection of appropriate primary cells, immortalized cell lines, or advanced 3D culture systems based on study requirements.
• Reliable Scientific Data: Studies conducted using validated methodologies, appropriate controls, and reproducible experimental workflows.
• Actionable Interpretation: Comprehensive data analysis with scientific interpretation to support research and development decisions.
What We Deliver
Receptor Binding Assays
Evaluate target affinity, selectivity, and ligand–receptor interactions using validated binding platforms. Typical outputs include Ki, Kd, IC50/EC50 and selectivity profiling to support target validation and lead optimisation.
Cytotoxicity & Cell Viability
Assess compound-induced effects on cellular health using MTT, Resazurin, ATP, LDH and related assays to establish therapeutic windows and early safety profiles.
Functional & Mechanism of Action Studies
Characterise biological pathways using reporter gene assays, biomarker analysis, cytokine profiling and functional cell-based assays to understand how a candidate produces its pharmacological effect.
2D & 3D Cell Models
Employ monolayer cultures, spheroids and co-culture models to generate physiologically relevant efficacy and toxicity data.
Enzyme & Biochemical Assays
Measure enzyme inhibition, activation and kinetics to support hit validation, SAR studies and off-target profiling.
Standards & Methods
Every study follows predefined protocols, qualified methods and appropriate experimental controls to ensure reproducibility and scientific integrity. Where applicable, studies are aligned with internationally recognised guidance and sponsor requirements.

In Vivo Pharmacology & Efficacy

In vivo pharmacology studies establish whether a therapeutic candidate demonstrates meaningful biological activity in a living system. Using disease-relevant animal models, these studies evaluate efficacy, characterize pharmacokinetic–pharmacodynamic (PK/PD) relationships, and generate the evidence needed to support preclinical decision-making and candidate progression.

Compliance: IAEC Approved • CPCSEA Registered • GLP-aligned
Why In Vivo Studies Matter
Promising in vitro activity is only the beginning of preclinical development. In vivo pharmacology determines whether that biological effect translates into a living system, where absorption, distribution, metabolism, immune responses, and tissue interactions collectively influence therapeutic performance. Well-designed in vivo studies provide the evidence required to evaluate efficacy, establish exposure–response relationships, optimize dose selection, and support progression into toxicology and clinical development.

At BioVeritas, studies are designed around clearly defined scientific questions rather than standardized templates. Every protocol integrates disease relevance, appropriate controls, meaningful endpoints, and statistical planning to generate reliable data that supports informed development decisions.

What We Deliver
Efficacy & Proof-of-Concept
Disease-relevant efficacy studies designed to demonstrate therapeutic activity and support candidate progression across oncology, metabolic disorders, CNS, inflammation, dermatology, and immunology.
PK/PD & Exposure Assessment
Studies integrating pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic endpoints to characterize exposure–response relationships, optimize dosing strategies, and strengthen translational confidence.
Biomarker & Endpoint Evaluation
Assessment using histopathology, imaging, circulating biomarkers, molecular biomarkers, behavioural assessments, and functional endpoints where appropriate.
Custom Model Development
Development or adaptation of disease models tailored to sponsor-specific biological questions when standard models are not sufficient.

Understanding How a Molecule Behaves in the Body
Pharmacokinetic (PK) and ADME studies evaluate how a therapeutic candidate is absorbed, distributed, metabolized, and eliminated after administration. These studies provide the quantitative evidence needed to understand systemic exposure, optimize dosing strategies, assess drug–drug interaction potential, and support safe progression from preclinical research to clinical development.
