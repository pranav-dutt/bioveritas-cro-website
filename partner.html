Commercial engagement

Three ways sponsors engage us.

Most sponsors start with a single milestone and expand. All three models run under one master services agreement with study-level work orders.

01

Full-programme partner

BioVeritas takes accountability for the research programme end to end — non-clinical through regulatory submission — with a single study director, one governance rhythm, and consolidated reporting.

02

Milestone specialist

Engage us for a defined milestone — a toxicology package, a bioanalytical method, a BA/BE study, a dossier module — with a fixed scope, timeline, and deliverable.

03

White-label CRO support

For consultancies and CROs needing scientific capacity: we deliver under your brand, to your templates, with confidentiality and non-solicitation contractually defined.

Academic & institutional

Research collaboration.

We collaborate with universities, institutes, and hospital research groups where the science is genuinely joint — shared design, shared authorship, shared credit.

  • Joint studies — co-designed protocols with defined contributions and data-sharing terms agreed in advance.
  • Co-authored publications — authorship and acknowledgement agreed at protocol stage, not at submission.
  • University tie-ups — student projects, dissertation supervision, and method-development collaborations.
  • Grant-funded work — CRO partner on funded proposals, including budgeting and letters of support.
