Device evidence is a risk argument, not a test list — the right package depends on materials, body contact, and duration, and is defended through a biological evaluation plan.

We start from the device description, materials, and contact profile, then build a risk-based biological evaluation plan under ISO 10993-1 that justifies which endpoints need testing and which can be addressed by existing data.

Testing is then executed through qualified partner laboratories under our quality oversight, and the output is assembled into a biological evaluation report a reviewer can follow end to end.

Test what the risk assessment demands

A justified biological evaluation plan routinely removes redundant endpoints — reducing cost and timeline without weakening the submission.

What we deliver

Device support covers evaluation planning, laboratory testing, and the documentation that carries it into a submission.

01

Biological evaluation planning

ISO 10993-1 risk-based biological evaluation plans, endpoint justification, and gap analysis against existing material data.

02

Biocompatibility testing

Cytotoxicity, irritation, sensitisation, acute systemic toxicity, pyrogenicity, haemocompatibility, and implantation studies.

03

Chemical characterisation

Extractables and leachables assessment, material characterisation, and toxicological risk assessment of identified compounds.

04

Performance & bench testing

Functional, mechanical, and usability bench studies designed around intended use and the claims being supported.

05

Clinical evaluation

Clinical evaluation reports, literature appraisal, post-market clinical follow-up planning, and clinical investigation support.

06

Regulatory support

CDSCO device licensing dossiers, technical files, and responses to reviewer queries for Indian and international markets.

How an engagement runs

Device engagements begin with the risk argument and end with a reviewable evidence file.

01 · Scoping

Device & contact profile

Classify the device, map body contact and duration, and review available material data.

02 · Design

Biological evaluation plan

Justify required endpoints, propose the test matrix, and align with the target market.

03 · Execution

Testing

Run testing through qualified laboratories under quality oversight and defined protocols.

04 · Analysis

Toxicological review

Interpret findings and assess residual risk against intended use.

05 · Reporting

Evaluation report

Deliver a biological evaluation report and submission-ready documentation.

Quality & data integrity

Work is conducted under quality-managed SOPs with ALCOA+ data-integrity principles. Raw data is captured contemporaneously, archived, and made available for sponsor and agency audit.

  • ISO 9001 quality-management system across the lab's operations.
  • Independent review of protocols, conduct, and final reports before release.
  • Traceable records — every result traces back to an explicit method and standard.
  • Confidential by default — mutual NDA available within 24 hours of request.
