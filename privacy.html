Who we are

BioVeritas CRO Private Limited (“BioVeritas”, “we”, “us”) is a contract research organization registered in India, with its office at Ground Floor, B-14, Sector-132, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. We are the data fiduciary for personal data collected through this website. Questions about this policy can be sent to bioveritascro@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, organisation, work email, and telephone number submitted through our enquiry, proposal, partnership, or careers forms.
  • Project information — the study brief, molecule or product details, phase, timelines, and any technical context you choose to share.
  • Recruitment information — résumé, qualifications, experience, and availability where you apply for a role or internship.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, collected through analytics.
  • Cookies — as described in our cookie policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a study proposal or quotation.
  • To perform contracted research services and communicate about live projects.
  • To evaluate job and internship applications.
  • To send scientific or regulatory updates where you have asked for them — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use sponsor project information for any purpose other than servicing your enquiry or contract.

Confidentiality of sponsor information

Technical and commercial information you send us in connection with a study is treated as confidential. It is shared only with the BioVeritas personnel scoping or performing the work, and with partner facilities where a study requires it — in that case under written confidentiality obligations no less protective than our own. A mutual non-disclosure agreement can be executed before technical detail is exchanged; request one and we will return it within 24 hours.

Legal basis

Where Indian law applies, we process personal data on the basis of your consent, the performance of a contract, our legitimate interest in operating a research business, and compliance with legal obligations. Where the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to a given processing activity, we act as data fiduciary accordingly. Where you are located in the EU or UK, processing is based on Article 6(1)(a), (b), (c) or (f) of the GDPR as applicable.

Retention

  • Enquiry and proposal correspondence — up to 3 years from last contact, unless a contract is signed.
  • Contracted study records — for the retention period agreed in the study contract, or as required by applicable regulation.
  • Job applications — 12 months from submission, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Analytics data — as configured in our analytics platform, typically up to 14 months.

Sharing and transfers

We share personal data with: service providers who host this website, deliver email, or provide analytics; qualified partner laboratories and clinical sites where a study requires it; professional advisers; and authorities where we are legally obliged to. Where data is transferred outside India, we rely on contractual protections with the recipient.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, update, or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, object to processing, or receive a copy in portable form. Write to bioveritascro@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Security

We apply access control, device and account security, and confidentiality obligations on personnel. Study data is segregated per sponsor. No method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure — please do not send highly sensitive material through the website forms; ask us for a secure transfer link instead.

Children

This website is intended for professional users. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The revision date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
