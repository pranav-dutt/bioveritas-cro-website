Manufacturing coordination

Evidence matched to the claim

Existing ingredient evidence can inform product development and scientific positioning. Finished-product claims should be supported by evidence appropriate to the specific formulation and intended claim.

Development anticipates manufacturing from the outset: formulation performance, process feasibility and scalability are considered early, so the product transfers cleanly to pilot and commercial production.

  • Preformulation: ICH Q8(R2) principles, physicochemical profile.
  • Formulation: Quality by Design (QbD) approach, where applicable.
  • Stability: ICH Q1 series, stability summary and shelf-life support.
  • Technology transfer: process documentation and transfer package.
