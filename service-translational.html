Attrition in clinical development is rarely a chemistry problem. It is usually a translation problem — a pre-clinical package that answered a question no clinician asked.

Translational research sits deliberately between our non-clinical and clinical service lines. It asks, before a study is run, what result would change a clinical decision — and designs backwards from that.

We build translational strategies that connect mechanism, exposure, biomarker, and endpoint into one argument, and revisit that argument as data arrives rather than at the end of the programme.

One argument, from model to endpoint

Mechanism, exposure, biomarker, and endpoint are documented as a single translational rationale — the narrative regulators look for in Module 2 and reviewers look for in a paper.

What we deliver

Translational support can run as a standalone strategic engagement or alongside an active pre-clinical or clinical programme.

01

Translational strategy

A written translational rationale linking target biology, model choice, exposure, biomarkers, and clinical endpoints for the whole programme.

02

Human-relevance assessment

Critical review of model systems, species selection, and target expression to test whether pre-clinical findings should be expected to translate.

03

PK/PD & dose translation

Allometric scaling, exposure–response modelling, and first-in-human dose justification bridging animal data to clinical protocols.

04

Biomarker strategy & qualification

Biomarker selection, fit-for-purpose assay development, and qualification plans for target engagement, pharmacodynamics, and safety.

05

Endpoint & design advice

Endpoint, estimand, and population selection for proof-of-mechanism and proof-of-concept studies, with statistical input from the outset.

06

Repositioning & lifecycle science

Mechanistic and evidence review to support new indications, combination rationales, and lifecycle extension programmes.

How an engagement runs

A translational engagement is short, senior, and decision-oriented.

01 · Scoping

Programme review

Review the existing pre-clinical and clinical evidence base and the decision it has to support.

02 · Design

Translational plan

Draft the translational rationale: models, exposure targets, biomarkers, and endpoints.

03 · Execution

Enabling studies

Run or coordinate the bridging studies and biomarker work the plan identifies.

04 · Analysis

Integration

Integrate pre-clinical and clinical data into a single exposure–effect interpretation.

05 · Reporting

Strategy document

Deliver a translational strategy suited to internal governance and Module 2 use.

Quality & data integrity

Translational conclusions are documented with their evidence base and assumptions stated explicitly, so reviewers can see what is measured and what is inferred.

  • Explicit assumptions — scaling factors and model limitations stated, not buried.
  • Traceable evidence — every claim linked to a study, dataset, or citation.
  • Independent review by a scientist outside the study team before release.
  • Confidential by default — mutual NDA available within 24 hours of request.
