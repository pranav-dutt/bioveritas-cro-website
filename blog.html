Publications we can support

We help sponsors, investigators and research teams turn completed study data into publications across four research areas.

Clinical research

  • Clinical study manuscripts
  • BA/BE study publications
  • Case reports and case series
  • Real-world evidence summaries

Preclinical research

  • Pharmacology and efficacy studies
  • Toxicology and safety findings
  • PK and ADME studies
  • ARRIVE-aligned reporting

Bioanalytical research

  • Method development and validation papers
  • Biomarker studies
  • LC–MS/MS and ligand-binding methods
  • Analytical technical notes

Nutraceutical & natural-product research

  • Ingredient and formulation studies
  • Safety and efficacy evidence
  • Standardisation and phytochemical work
  • Review articles on natural products

Publication-support services

  1. Publication planningTarget audience, journal shortlist and timelines agreed with the authors.
  2. Data analysis & interpretationStatistical analysis and scientific interpretation of completed study data.
  3. Manuscript writing & editingDrafting and editing to the journal’s author guidelines and reporting standards (CONSORT, ARRIVE, STROBE as applicable).
  4. Submission supportFormatting, cover letters and submission to the selected journal or conference.
  5. Reviewer responsePoint-by-point responses and revisions after peer review.

Authorship follows ICMJE criteria and remains with the study investigators and sponsor. Publication support does not guarantee acceptance by any journal.
