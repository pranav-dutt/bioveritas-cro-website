What we deliver

Biomarker testing is part of our broader bioanalytical capability, not a separate service. One team develops, validates and runs both, from method development through regulated sample analysis.

Bioanalytical testing (LC–MS/MS)
LC–MS/MS and ligand-binding (including ELISA) assay development for small molecules, peptides, and biologics.
Method validation
Accuracy, precision, selectivity, linearity, LLOQ, recovery, and stability under ICH M10 – Bioanalytical Method Validation and Study Sample Analysis, and ICH Q2(R2) where applicable.
Sample analysis
Study-sample analysis through qualified GLP-compliant partner laboratories, as applicable, with incurred-sample reanalysis where required.
Stability studies
Biological matrix stability: bench-top, freeze–thaw, long-term and processed-sample stability, as applicable under ICH M10.
Impurity profiling
Identification and quantitation of impurities and degradants.
Ligand-binding assays, including ELISA
Immunoassay development, qualification and validation for biologics, anti-drug antibodies and protein biomarkers, according to intended use.
Biomarker analysis
Biomarker and ligand-binding assays are developed, qualified or validated according to their intended use and applicable regulatory requirements, including ICH M10 where applicable to regulated bioanalytical methods.

How an engagement runs

A consistent five-stage flow keeps every engagement aligned with regulatory intent, from feasibility through a decision-ready report.

01 · Scoping

Feasibility & Gap Analysis

Map existing data to your target submission; identify gaps, endpoints, and study-design options.

02 · Design

Protocol & Plan

Draft an OECD/ICH-aligned protocol with a statistical/analysis plan and clear acceptance criteria.

03 · Execution

Conduct

Studies run through qualified and appropriately accredited/GLP-compliant partner facilities, as applicable, with BioVeritas scientific coordination and sponsor-facing oversight.

04 · Analysis

Review & Interpret

Quality-reviewed data and documentation, biostatistics where relevant, and scientific interpretation, not just raw delivery.

05 · Reporting

Decision-Ready Report

A structured report and data package prepared with audit and agency review in mind.

Quality & data integrity

Work is conducted under quality-managed SOPs with ALCOA+ data-integrity principles. Raw data is captured contemporaneously, archived, and made available for sponsor and agency audit.

  • Qualified partners: regulated work is conducted through qualified and appropriately accredited partner facilities, as applicable to the study.
  • Independent review of protocols, conduct, and final reports before release.
  • Traceable records: every result traces back to an explicit method and standard.
  • Confidential by default: mutual NDA support, typically within 24 hours.
