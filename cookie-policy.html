What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies — local storage, pixels, and session identifiers — perform comparable functions. Together they let a site remember preferences and understand how it is being used.

Third-party cookies

Analytics cookies are set by Google Analytics 4 on our behalf. If you view an embedded map or an external video, that provider may set its own cookies under its own policy. We do not embed social-media tracking pixels.

How to control cookies

  • Browser settings — every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of this site.
  • Analytics opt-out — you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block analytics cookies in your browser.
  • Do Not Track — we do not respond to Do Not Track signals, as there is no common standard for doing so; we simply keep tracking to the minimum described above.

Changes

If we add or remove a cookie category we will update this page and its revision date. Related reading: our privacy policy and terms of use.
